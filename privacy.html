Privacy and AI use

Last updated 19 November 2026.

Who I am

Suhaimi Zainal Shah, trading as AI Benar Advisory, London, United Kingdom. Contact: suhaimizainalshah@gmail.com. I am the data controller for the personal data described here.

This website

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other companies except an anonymous counter (Abacus) that records page visits and button clicks as totals. The counter sets no cookies and counts nothing if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control. The AI rules check runs entirely in your browser; your answers are not sent anywhere unless you choose to email them to me using the link in the results.

When you contact me

If you email me or book a call, I use your name, email and what you tell me to reply and to prepare a proposal. The lawful basis is taking steps towards a contract at your request (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). I keep enquiries for up to two years, and client records for six years for tax purposes. Booking calls uses Calendly, which processes your booking under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how I use it, by emailing me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

How I use AI

I use AI tools to help with research, drafting and checking. A person, me, reviews everything before it reaches a client. I never put client personal data or confidential material into AI tools unless the client has agreed and the tool’s terms protect it, and I never use client material to train AI.
