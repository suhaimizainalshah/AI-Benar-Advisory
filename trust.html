Trust and security

How I look after your information. Last updated 28 November 2026.

What I hold

Only what the work needs: your contact details, what we agree, and the documents you choose to share. I don’t ask for access to your systems, and I don’t need it for guidance or reviews.

How it’s protected

  • Work is done on my own devices, which are encrypted, kept up to date, and locked when not in use.
  • Accounts that hold client material use multi-factor sign-in.
  • Your documents are kept only as long as the engagement needs, then deleted, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • If something goes wrong that affects your information, I’ll tell you promptly, with what happened and what I’m doing about it.

AI and your information

I never put your personal data or confidential material into an AI tool unless you’ve agreed. A person, me, checks everything before it reaches you. The detail is in the privacy notice.

My own tools

The free tools on aibenar.dev work on your device: what you check or type stays in your browser, with no accounts and no tracking. The governance trackers count visits anonymously, and not at all if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Certifications

Current status: not yet certified. I’m working towards Cyber Essentials, the UK government-backed scheme, and an ISO/IEC 42001 credential. This page will say so when each is complete, with the certificate number.

Questions

If your organisation needs a security questionnaire answered or a data processing agreement signed, ask: I’ll answer it honestly, including where the answer is “no” or “not yet”.
