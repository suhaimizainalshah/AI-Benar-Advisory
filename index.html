The dates that matter

Several AI laws already apply. These are the next deadlines my clients plan around.

  1. 22 January 2026South KoreaAI Basic Act in force, including generative AI notices and labels.
  2. 1 March 2026VietnamLaw on Artificial Intelligence in force for new systems.
  3. 2 August 2026European UnionAI Act transparency duties apply: tell people when they talk to AI.
  4. 2 December 2026European UnionMachine-readable marking of AI content for systems already on the market.
  5. January 2027South KoreaThe one-year pause on AI Basic Act fines is expected to end.
  6. March 2027VietnamGrace period ends for AI systems that were already running, in most sectors.
  7. September 2027VietnamGrace period ends in healthcare, education and finance.
  8. 2 December 2027European UnionHigh-risk duties begin for AI in hiring, education, credit and essential services.

Breaches of the EU AI Act’s transparency duties can be fined up to €15 million or 3% of worldwide annual turnover.

Common questions

Do I need to comply if my company isn’t in the EU or Vietnam?

Often, yes. The EU AI Act and Vietnam’s AI Law reach organisations abroad whose AI is used in those countries, and South Korea’s AI Basic Act covers foreign AI businesses serving Korean users. The free check above gives a first answer.

Is this legal advice?

No. I help you understand what the rules require and put practical measures in place. For a formal legal opinion, I’ll refer you to a partner law firm.

Can you work in my time zone?

Yes. I work with clients from Europe to Southeast Asia, and schedule calls for your morning or mine.

How do you handle my data?

I only collect what a project needs, keep it for as long as the engagement requires, and never use client material to train AI. This website sets no cookies and uses only anonymous counts.
